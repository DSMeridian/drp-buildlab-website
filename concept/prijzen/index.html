Questions about price

The answers to what clients ask most.

What does a website from DRP BuildLab cost?

Every website is made to measure, so the price depends on the work that goes into it. For new businesses, or those updating an existing site, our starter package begins at €499. For larger projects we make a quote to measure. Maintenance is optional: €29 a month or €250 a year.

Who is the €499 starter package for?

For business owners who have just started without a website, or who want their existing website turned into a modern, professional one.

What does maintenance cost?

Maintenance is optional and costs €29 a month, or €250 a year, which saves you €98. For that we keep your website safe, fast and optimised for search. You are not tied in: you can cancel any month, and the website always stays yours.

How quickly will my website be online?

As soon as we have your texts, photos and logo, we can move fast. At the start we agree on a delivery date that fits your project, and we keep to it. Along the way you get a preview to approve, so you are never left waiting.

How does a quote to measure work?

For larger projects we first discuss what you need in a free introductory call. Then we make a personal quote based on the work involved: pages, features, integrations and maintenance. No hidden costs: you know in advance what you will pay.
