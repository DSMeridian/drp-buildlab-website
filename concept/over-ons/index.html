Our story

Every business deserves a fair chance to be found online.

It started with a frustration. We saw good businesses that were almost invisible online, and agencies that asked €5,000 for a website that then took months to arrive.

So we built DRP BuildLab: a studio that believes every business deserves a website made to measure. Affordable, honest, and priced on the work that actually goes into it.
