The peoplebehind yourwebsite.

Who we are, why we started and what you can expect from us.

Our story

Every business deserves a fair chance to be found online.

It started with a frustration. We saw good businesses that were almost invisible online, and agencies that asked €5,000 for a website that then took months to arrive.

So we built DRP BuildLab: a studio that believes every business deserves a website made to measure. Affordable, honest, and priced on the work that actually goes into it. We work remotely from Belgium, so where you are does not decide whether we can build for you.

What we stand for

Three promises behind every project.

Honesty above all

What we promise, we deliver. No hidden costs and no vague quotes.

Care when you want it

Maintenance is optional: €29 a month or €250 a year. Take it and your site stays safe, up to date and findable, without you having to think about it.

Worldwide, built personally

We build for businesses wherever their customers are searching. You get the same small team, wherever you are.

Four people, one contact

No ticket system and no rotating account managers. This is everyone who works on your website.

Why DRP BuildLab

For businesses that want to be online without the hassle and without surprises.

One fixed contact

You have our names and our number, from the first call to years after launch.

Fair prices

No hidden costs. Agreed in advance, so you know exactly what you pay.

Made for phones

Every DRP BuildLab site works on every device, because that is where your customers find you.

Built to be found

SEO is part of the build, so Google can find, understand and index your site.

A partner for the long run

After launch we are still there. Not a one-off supplier, but a team you keep.
