What partners ask

The short answers.

Who can become a partner?

Anyone with an audience of business owners: social media creators, consultants, accountants, coaches, networkers. You don’t need a huge reach, just an audience for whom a custom website makes sense. We review every request personally.

When do I earn my commission?

Only when the client actually pays the invoice in the client portal. A request or an approved quote earns nothing yet: we pay on what comes in, not on what is promised.

What is the 5% calculated on?

On the amount the client actually pays, so after the 10% discount your link gave them, and excluding VAT. If a project is invoiced in instalments, you earn on every paid instalment.

What if someone uses two partner links?

The first link counts. A link does not expire: whoever clicked it stays linked to that partner until we revoke the link, and a later link does not overwrite it.

How do I track what I have earned?

You get a dashboard with your clicks, requests, paid invoices and open commission. What you earn in a calendar month is paid out by bank transfer during the following month.
