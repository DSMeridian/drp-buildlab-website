The brief

Petra Vanhandenhoven helps entrepreneurs take the right steps with their business, through e-books, workshops and one-to-one guidance. She needed one place where all of that comes together, and that feels as personal as her coaching.

What we did

We designed and built the site around Petra herself: a strong editorial headline, her portrait, and a clear route to each offer — e-books, workshops, guidance and her Dreamteam.

It works as well on a phone as on a desktop. We still maintain it, so updates and new offers go live without Petra having to touch any code.
