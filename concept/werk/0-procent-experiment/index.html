The brief

0% Experiment is a digital workbook that guides you through 30 days without alcohol, with one question each day. It is curious, not preachy: you are the test subject and the researcher.

What we did

We designed a bright, confident site with a neon accent and big type that states the idea in one line: take one thing away and see what is left. It explains what the workbook is, how it works and who it is for, then leads straight to starting the experiment.
