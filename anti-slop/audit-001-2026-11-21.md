# Antislop audit 001 (21 Nov 2026)

Mode: AFTER (audit only, nothing changed). Scope: the redesign as built on
`release/2026-11-20-black-friday` (homepage, contact/quote builder, pricing,
the Black Friday additions, shared header/footer), checked in English and
Dutch, with translation checks across all 12 languages.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM,
Quality Locks = LOW. "Cost" says what a fix touches: **CSS** changes no text
and needs no retranslation; **copy** changes English strings, so all 11
languages need new hand translations (DeepL quota is out).

## Findings

### HIGH

**1. Italic tags split or misplaced by translation: 43 strings in 11 languages** (C-4 Resilience; raised to HIGH because it is a visible defect)
The italic word is a separate `<em>` inside each heading, and DeepL moved it
around. Examples: nl `Laten we <em>uw website</em> bouwen<em>.</em>` (the
italic full stop that reads as "bouwen ."), nl `<em>Met</em> wie wij
<em>samenwerken</em>` ("With" in italics), nl hero `om<em> klanten…` (space
inside the italic), de `erstellen<em>.</em>`, ja ends headings with an
italic `<em>。</em>`. Per language: nl 5, fr 3, de 4, it 6, es 4, pl 5, pt 3,
id 2, ja 6, ar 4, af 1.
Cost: CSS (fixed for free by finding 4) or cache edits.

**2. Em dashes in visible text** (R-02)
English: 2 (Petra case study `did`, Redesign service intro "We keep what
works — your content…"). Added by translation where the English has none:
es 2, pt 1, id 3.
Cost: copy for the 2 English strings; the 6 translated ones are cache edits
(no key change).

**3. No design direction on file** (R-37)
There is no DESIGN.md or brand guide, so the dials (ENERGY / RHYTHM / MOTION)
and the reasons behind the type, colour and layout choices are not written
anywhere. Findings 4 to 12 all come back to this: without a stated
direction the defaults filled in.
Cost: a one-page DESIGN.md agreed with you; no site change by itself.

### MEDIUM

**4. "Normal words + italic blue words" heading formula on 33 headings** (R-06, R-31)
One rule (`.h1 em,.h2 em{font-style:italic;font-weight:900;color:…}`) styles
nearly every heading the same way: "Wat wij *bouwen*", "Waarom klanten bij
ons *blijven*", "Meer website, *zelfde prijs.*", "Laten we *uw website*
bouwen". Used once it is an accent; used everywhere it is the template.
Cost: CSS only (keep the `<em>` in the markup so no translation key changes;
style it as plain text everywhere, or keep it on one heading as the single
deliberate accent).

**5. Eyebrow pill above headlines** (R-09)
`.deal-tag` (pill + dot + monospace uppercase "BLACK FRIDAY · TOT MAANDAG 30
NOVEMBER", added by me on 20 Nov) and `.tr-badge` (pill + inset border
"Official Tinrate reseller"). The deal date is real information; the pill,
dot, mono and uppercase are decoration on top of it.
Cost: CSS (restyle as plain text line).

**6. Equal-length, equal-shape benefit items** (R-14, C-3)
"Waarom klanten bij ons blijven": 4 rows, each a 3-5 word title + one
sentence (149-233 characters). Deal offers: 2 identical cards (143-150
characters). Partner cards: 3 identical cards. The even word counts read as
generated.
Cost: copy (rewrite unevenly, merge or drop rows) + translation; or layout
only (CSS/markup) if the text stays.

**7. Coloured left stripe on the deal notes** (Part 1 "Colored Left Stripe")
`.deal-note{border-inline-start:3px solid …}` in the forms and on the
pricing card, added by me. Decoration only. (The blog blockquote stripe has
a purpose and stays.)
Cost: CSS.

**8. Radial glow behind the hero** (R-01)
`.hero::before` is a blue radial orb on the dark hero; 6 radial gradients in
total.
Cost: CSS.

**9. Every block slides up on scroll** (R-19)
25 elements on the homepage carry `.rv` (translateY 34px over 1.1 s). It
respects reduced motion, but it is the same fade-up everywhere with no
stated purpose.
Cost: CSS/JS (keep it for one moment, or drop it).

**10. Decorative arrows** (R-08)
Button arrows are already hidden by CSS. Still visible: the circled "→" on
every service row and "Back to top ↑".
Cost: CSS.

### LOW

**11. Uniform section rhythm** (R-05)
Sections "Ons werk", "Wat wij bouwen", "Waarom klanten bij ons blijven",
"Met wie wij samenwerken" share one composition (`.sec-head`: big H2 left,
one-line sub right, then a list/grid), and the only change between sections
is the background alternating dark / white / grey. Fixing this properly is a
layout redesign of the homepage and needs the RHYTHM dial from finding 3.
Cost: markup + CSS; little or no copy.

**12. Pill shape as the default** (R-11)
Buttons, chips, badges, the sticky "Get a free quote" button, the language
switcher: `border-radius:99px`/`50%` 26 times. Cards (22px) and inputs
(12px) do vary.
Cost: CSS.

**13. Numbered process steps** (R-05 "How it works")
Homepage CTA: 4 numbered steps; contact page: 3 numbered steps ("U stuurt
het formulier / Wij bellen u / U beslist").
Cost: markup/CSS, possibly copy.

**14. Typeface and dark default without a written reason** (R-06, R-21)
Body font is Geist (named as an AI default pick); display is condensed
uppercase Archivo, which does carry identity. The page is dark by default
with light sections. Both may be right for the brand, but the reason is not
written down (ties to finding 3).
Cost: DESIGN.md line, or a font change (CSS).

**15. Partner quotes read as slogans** (R-18 presentation, not content)
The quotes are real (confirmed by you), so they stay. They show in English
on every language, in quote marks with no speaker, so visitors read them as
taglines. Naming the person (and role) would make them read as what they
are.
Cost: markup; needs the names from the team.
