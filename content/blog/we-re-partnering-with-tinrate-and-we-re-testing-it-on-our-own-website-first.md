Nobody wants to read an entire website anymore. They want to ask a question and get an answer, quickly.

That is why I sat down with Gunther Ghysels, the founder of [Tinrate](https://tinrate.com/), to talk about where we could work together. The result: we are becoming a Tinrate partner, and before we bring it to a single client, we are running it on our own website first.

## What Tinrate does

[Tinrate](https://tinrate.com/) is an AI startup from Ghent, built around one idea: connecting knowledge. Its main product is the marketplace where you can find all sorts of expertise and advise and their newest product is the **Concierge**.

The Concierge lives on your website and answers visitors on the spot, from your own knowledge. It qualifies them, and it gets every request to the right person in your organisation. If a conversation needs a call, it can book one, free or paid. No more "we’ll get back to you", and no more manually forwarding what lands in the info@ inbox. Also connections with every CRM is included.

Apart from conversion Tinrate concierge also gives customer data insights, now you really know what your customers need or what the trends are.

## Why this fits how people search now

Our customers no longer browse menus and skim pages. Just like with GEO (generative engine optimization), they want to ask a question, simplify the process, and speed it up. A website that makes people dig for the answer loses to one that simply answers.

## What sets Tinrate apart in our opinion

This morning, our head software engineer, Lathif Sihab Dewantoro, shared his view with me. What stands out to him is that Tinrate distinguishes itself with a workflow configuration that is not technical. The people who know your customers best can decide how questions are handled and who they get routed to, without waiting for a developer.

That was the deciding factor for us. A tool that only engineers can configure stays a side project. A tool that the whole team can shape actually gets used.

## Why we test it on ourselves first

We will not recommend something we have not lived with. So the Concierge is going onto our own website, and we will be looking at:

- how well it answers real visitor questions
- how much faster visitors get what they came for
- how easy the configuration is for people who are not engineers
- what needs to improve before we roll it out to clients
- how it affects lead conversion and workflow automation

## What’s next

We will share what we learn along the way, including what does not work. In the meantime, try it on our website and tell us what you think. And if you would like to see the Concierge on your own site, get in touch.
