Frequently asked questions

What partners
usually ask.

Who can become a marketing partner?

Anyone with an audience of business owners: social media creators, consultants, accountants, coaches, networkers. You do not need a large reach — you need an audience for whom a custom website makes sense. We look at every application personally.

When do I earn my commission?

Only when the client actually pays the invoice in the client portal. A request, or even an approved quote, earns nothing yet: we pay out on what comes in, not on what was promised. That way there is never a commission standing against an invoice that never gets paid.

What is the 5% calculated on?

On the amount the client actually pays — so after the 10% discount your link earned them, and excluding VAT. On a project invoiced in stages, you earn on every stage that is paid.

What if someone arrives through two partner links?

The first link counts. A link stays valid for 90 days from the click, and during that time a later one does not overwrite it. That stops anyone taking over an existing recommendation by getting in at the last moment.

How do I track what I have earned?

You get access to a dashboard showing your clicks, requests, paid invoices and outstanding commission. Whatever is earned in a calendar month is paid out by bank transfer during the month after.
